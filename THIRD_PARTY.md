# External methods and data

Original methods and papers are credited in the protocol, theory and assessment documents. No ownership of those methods or faithful author-code reproduction is implied.

Dependencies are installed separately, not vendored: [NumPy](https://github.com/numpy/numpy), [SciPy](https://github.com/scipy/scipy), [scikit-learn](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn), [Matplotlib](https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib), and additional packages listed in requirements. Official datasets are downloaded separately and verified by the project; their original terms apply. No raw datasets or third-party paper PDFs are redistributed. Included figures/tables are this project's own outputs.
