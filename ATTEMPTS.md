# Attempt history

1. Searched recent routing literature before building the public-data pilot. Found SaveRouter (September 2026), which directly covers upfront feedback expenditure and amortized payback. Rejected the broad lifecycle framing as a novelty claim. Narrowed to uncertainty in prospective quality/payback decisions; explicitly required a useful empirical finding beyond standard inference.
2. Downloaded official RouterBench zero-shot outcomes, pinned revision and SHA256. Inspected schema, not aggregate quality. Reserved final/fresh normalized prompt groups before fitting. The schema-inspected first prompt is excluded from held-out pools.
3. Wrote PROTOCOL.md and saved its hash. Initial execution stopped before fitting because training-only cached math scores were fractional. Retained scores unchanged, replaced the proposed binomial diagnostic with a bounded-mean bound, and saved a corrected pre-run hash. No metric or held-out outcome was used to choose a favorable score transformation.
4. Completed the 43-policy pilot over 18 workload/seed/budget runs. The original primary gate failed: zero 10K-query deployments were approved with fresh dense feedback costs. Longer-horizon law outcomes appeared sensitive to uncertainty. Archived every result.
5. Added selected-pair calibration acquisition as a labeled engineering sensitivity: independent validation of a frozen policy does not require all 11 model calls. Verified that original dense decisions reproduced exactly. Did not change the original primary gate or learned policies.
6. Added a stronger simple control motivated by RouterBench's Zero Router: 60 predefined reference/alternative mixtures, giving 103 candidate policies. Repeated all 18 runs and sensitivities. The selected law policies became simple mixtures, eliminating the earlier adverse point decisions at the default 100K-query configuration. This weakens the proposed empirical novelty claim rather than supporting it.
7. The final experiment contains 2,592 correlated scenario/rule assessments, not 2,592 independent deployments. Five tests passed. Split/cost/provenance audits passed, tables and figures were generated, and final evaluation was refused by the guard.

Outcome: no qualified workload under the predefined opportunity gate; no new method/theorem; no robust independent-benchmark empirical contribution. Fresh/final pools remain unscored. The study is delivered as a reproducible engineering assessment, not a complete novel-method paper.
